Putting Fair Returns Directly Into The Hands Of Growers

In traditional grocery logistics, small family farmers often receive less than 14 cents on the dollar. Agro’s cooperative model flips the balance completely, returning up to 78% of retail sales straight to the farm gate.

This economic stability allows our growers to invest in perennial cover crops, invest in solar pump systems, and pass down chemical-free land to their children with pride.

A Day In The Life Of An Agro Farmer

Follow the meticulous rhythm of natural agriculture.

05:00 AM

Dawn Dew Picking

Vegetables are plucked when morning dew preserves peak cell hydration and cellular crunch.

08:30 AM

Solar Cold Transfer

Crops are sorted into breathable crates and placed in on-farm solar chill units within 90 minutes.

01:00 PM

Compost & Soil Inoculation

Spraying aerobic microbial tea onto harvested beds to replenish nitrogen naturally before replanting.

06:00 PM

Refrigerated Dispatch

Our fleet collects crates from regional farm hubs for night sorting and next-morning doorstep delivery.
