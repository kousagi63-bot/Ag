Origin & Legacy

25+ Years of Preserving Soil, Seed & Community

In 1998, Agro started with 12 acres of family land and a firm conviction: that modern industrial agriculture was depleting essential soil microbiomes and stripping natural produce of taste and vital nutrition.

Today, our certified organic cooperative connects over 1,200 small-scale generational farmsteads across the nation. By pooling decentralized cold storage, fair trade pricing, and direct-to-consumer logistics, we give smallholders the power to farm without synthetic chemicals.

Guiding Principles

Our 4 Pillars of Sustainable Cultivation

How we harmonize traditional agronomy wisdom with high-tech organic science.

Living Microbiome Soil

We nurture billions of beneficial microorganisms per square foot via aerobic vermicomposting and zero deep-till practices.

Closed-Loop Water Stewardship

Solar-powered micro-drip irrigation systems that reduce regional water consumption by 62% compared to flood canals.

Heirloom Genetic Bank

Preserving over 400 ancestral plant and vegetable seed varieties naturally resistant to local droughts and pests.

Ethical Human Labor

Living wages, healthcare support, and continuing educational agronomy workshops for every farmer in our network.
