Commercial

Wholesale & Culinary Supply Inquiries

We partner with over 200 Michelin-starred and farm-to-table restaurants nationwide.

What are the minimum volume requirements for wholesale deliveries?
Our wholesale distribution desk accepts recurring weekly orders starting at a minimum of $250. Orders are chilled and dispatched in eco-crates with next-morning delivery.
Do you offer custom heirloom planting for specific restaurant menus?
Yes! Our agronomists collaborate with executive chefs during the winter planning season to reserve dedicated rows of rare heritage squash, edible flowers, and specific microgreens.
Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on our harvest delivery, ordering, and organic standards.

How fresh is the produce when delivered to my door?
All our fruits and vegetables are harvested within 24 hours of your delivery date. They are moved into temperature-regulated solar chilled transit and delivered directly to your doorstep in insulated, compostable crates.
Are all products in the Agro Marketplace 100% certified organic?
Yes, every single farm in our cooperative is audited against strict USDA Organic, Demeter Biodynamic, or Non-GMO Project standards with mandatory seasonal soil sample screenings.
Can I customize or skip a weekly harvest subscription basket?
Absolutely! You have full flexibility. Log in to your account up to 24 hours prior to your delivery day to swap produce items or skip deliveries without penalty.
